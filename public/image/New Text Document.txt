1. Poppins (Best Match)
This is likely the closest match. it has the same circular "o" and clean, open geometric shapes seen in the headings.

Where to find it: Google Fonts - Poppins

Usage: Use Bold (700) for "Hello, I'm..." and Regular (400) for the body text.

2. Montserrat
Another great choice that is slightly more "technical" and structured. It’s very common in UI/UX design portfolios.

Where to find it: Google Fonts - Montserrat

3. Inter
If you want something that looks a bit more "app-like" and is extremely easy to read on mobile screens (like the phone mockup on the right), Inter is the industry standard.

Where to find it: Google Fonts - Inter

How to use them like the image:
To get the exact look, follow this hierarchy:

Main Heading: Poppins, Bold, size 32px - 40px.

Sub-headings (About Me, My Skills): Poppins, Semi-Bold, size 20px - 24px.

Body Text: Poppins (or Inter), Regular, size 14px - 16px with a light grey color (e.g., #555555).
